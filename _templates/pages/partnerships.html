The Global Ecosystem Behind GRG

GRG is the headquarters and scientific lead of DIPY — but DIPY is an international project. Partnering with GRG means partnering with the core of a worldwide open-source community.

GRG Headquarters

DIPY — Diffusion Imaging in Python

The world's largest open-source library for diffusion MRI analysis. Originally founded by Prof. Garyfallidis at the University of Cambridge, DIPY is today maintained by an international team spanning five continents. GRG remains the scientific lead and primary development hub — giving our partners direct access to the people who design the algorithms, not just the documentation.

FURY & THETAN

Beyond DIPY, GRG leads FURY — a high-performance 3D scientific visualization framework — and THETAN, a next-generation machine learning framework targeting state-of-the-art performance across neuroimaging and signal processing domains (release planned Fall 2026).
