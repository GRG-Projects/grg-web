GRG Industry & Partnership Ecosystem

Accelerate your neuroimaging pipelines with direct access to the innovators behind DIPY, FURY, and THETAN.

While our core software remains completely open-source and free for the world, the Garyfallidis Research Group provides structured premium partnerships for academic labs and corporate teams requiring enterprise-grade optimization, sequence validation, and clinical trial compliance.

Why Partner with GRG?

Bridging cutting-edge computational neuroengineering and real-world industrial applications, GRG partnerships give your team an unfair advantage.

Eliminate Engineering Bottlenecks

Direct access to core maintainers of DIPY, FURY, and THETAN to optimize processing pipelines, configuration tuning, and throughput — eliminating the months your team would otherwise spend reverse-engineering undocumented internals.

Sequence Validation

Expert consulting on optimal diffusion acquisition strategies — multishell b-value schemes, directional resolution for crossing fibers, and protocol harmonization across scanner platforms — grounded in two decades of hands-on MRI research.

Regulatory & Compliance Support

Algorithmic documentation, validation datasets, and reproducibility evidence essential for FDA submissions and clinical trial compliance. Leverage an R1 university research group's institutional credibility for your regulatory pathway.

Include GRG in Your Grant

GRG has a strong track record as a funded collaborator on NIH and NSF grants. Adding us to your proposal brings computational neuroimaging expertise, validated open-source infrastructure, and Indiana University's institutional credibility directly into your research team.

20+ Years of NIH & NSF-funded research
3 Active open-source ecosystems (DIPY, FURY, THETAN)
R1 Indiana University — Carnegie R1 research institution

What We Bring to Your Proposal

  • Established computational neuroimaging pipeline (DIPY) already cited in hundreds of peer-reviewed publications
  • Expertise in diffusion MRI, tractography, and brain connectivity analysis
  • Machine learning and scientific visualization capabilities (FURY, THETAN)
  • A multidisciplinary team spanning ISE, neuroscience, and clinical imaging
  • Proven ability to deliver reproducible, open-source software artifacts required by NIH data-sharing mandates

What GRG Can Provide for Your Application

  • Letter of Support — signed by Prof. Garyfallidis, outlining GRG's commitment and role
  • NIH Biosketch — current biosketch for Prof. Garyfallidis and key personnel
  • Facilities & Resources statement — describing GRG compute infrastructure and IU HPC access
  • Subaward / Subcontract setup — IU Sponsored Research Services handles all subcontract paperwork
  • Scope of Work draft — tailored to your specific aims and budget period

Common Collaboration Mechanisms

  • NIH R01 / R21 subaward — GRG listed as subcontractor with dedicated budget
  • NSF collaborative research — multi-PI or subaward structure through IU Sponsored Programs
  • Consultant role — lighter engagement, no subcontract needed
  • Co-investigator — for deeper, multi-year research partnerships where GRG leads a specific aim
  • Data management plan contribution — GRG expertise in open-data and FAIR neuroimaging standards
Ready to collaborate on a grant? Contact us early — ideally 4–6 weeks before your submission deadline — so we have time to prepare the necessary documents and coordinate with IU Sponsored Research Services. Email workshop@dipy.org with your funding opportunity number and target submission date.
